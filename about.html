Our Story

Who We Are

Colourstreak is a creative and marketing solutions company based in Kolkata, India, specializing in graphic design, digital marketing, website development, signage, and digital printing services. We help businesses build strong brand identities and grow their presence across both digital and physical platforms.

With a team of creative designers, marketers, and technical experts, we combine creative thinking, strategic planning, and modern technology to deliver impactful solutions for our clients. From developing compelling brand visuals to building functional websites and executing effective marketing campaigns, we focus on creating work that drives real business results.

What We Do
